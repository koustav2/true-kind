Before you give

What we'll tell you, unprompted

Transparency isn't a page on a website. It's what we send you afterwards.

  1. We'll tell you

    Which program it went to

    Contributions are assigned to a named program, not a general pool, unless you specifically ask us to allocate it where it's most needed.

  2. We'll tell you

    What share reached the field

    We publish the split between program spend and admin, audit and reporting — and we don't quietly reclassify overheads to make it look better.

  3. We'll tell you

    What didn't work

    If the program you funded underperformed, that appears in the report too. You'll hear it from us before you hear it anywhere else.
