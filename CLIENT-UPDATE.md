# True Kind Foundation — Website Update

**Site:** https://truekind.truehr.co.in
**Date:** 19 August 2026

---

Dear Team,

The latest round of work on the True Kind Foundation website is complete and
live. Below is a summary of what has changed, followed by the content we need
from your side to finish the site properly.

## 1. You can now edit the website yourself

The largest addition is a content management system. You no longer need to come
back to us for wording changes, new photographs, or updated figures.

**Where:** https://truekind.truehr.co.in/portal/admin/cms
**Access:** your existing admin sign-in

Inside, you will find every page listed down the left-hand side. Select a page
and you can edit all of its text — headings, paragraphs, button labels, link
destinations, contact details, bank details and registration numbers.

A few points worth knowing:

- **"Header & footer" is shared.** Editing the phone number or address there
  updates it on all nine pages at once, so there is no risk of one page falling
  out of date.
- **Only what you change is stored.** Anything you leave alone continues to
  display the existing wording, so partial edits are perfectly safe.
- **Each field can be reset** to its original wording if an edit does not read
  the way you expected.
- **After saving, refresh the public page** with Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a
  Mac) to see the change. An ordinary refresh may show a cached copy.

## 2. Images and video

A media library now sits alongside the editor, at
https://truekind.truehr.co.in/portal/admin/cms/media

You can upload photographs and replace any image on the site. Eight video slots
have also been prepared — on the homepage, About Us, Our Work, Our Impact,
Donate, Volunteer, the Chairperson's Message and Press.

Each video slot accepts **either**:

- a video file uploaded directly (MP4, WebM or MOV, up to 200 MB), or
- a YouTube or Vimeo link pasted in.

We would recommend the YouTube or Vimeo route for anything longer than about a
minute — it costs nothing to serve and plays reliably on mobile connections.

A video slot with nothing in it stays completely hidden, so the pages look
finished whether or not you use them.

## 3. Design refresh

The colour scheme has been rebuilt around the four colours in your logo — green,
blue, purple and red — so the site and the mark now read as one identity. The
background has moved from cream to a cleaner white and light grey.

The four percentage dials on the homepage and the Our Impact page have been
redesigned. They now animate as the reader scrolls to them, with the figure
counting up as the ring fills.

One deliberate change: the fourth dial, "Would recommend us", was previously
shown in red. Red conventionally signals a problem, and that figure is the
strongest in the row, so it now uses the brand's teal instead. Red remains on
the donation buttons, where urgency is the intention.

All text and colour combinations across the site have been checked against the
WCAG AA accessibility standard for contrast, and all pass.

## 4. Housekeeping

The temporary notes we had left on the pages during the build — lines beginning
"Awaiting your…" and similar — have all been removed. The site now reads as a
finished publication to any visitor.

Routine security and performance hardening was also carried out across the
portal at the same time.

### Content still to supply

1. **Founding story** — the year the Foundation was established, the founders'
   names, and the first programme you ran.
2. **Board members** — full names and photographs for the Chairperson, Vice
   Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer. The roles and responsibilities are
   already written; only the names and photographs are missing.
3. **Chairperson's message** — the Chairperson's name and photograph.
4. **Participant quotes** — the Voices section carries four quotes. We need the
   real quotations, together with written consent from each person and the form
   of their name they are comfortable publishing.
5. **Programme photographs** — the five programme cards currently use
   illustrations. Real photographs from the field would considerably strengthen
   these pages.
6. **Donation amounts** — the Donate page shows three indicative figures
   (₹1,500, ₹6,000 and ₹25,000) against what each covers. Please confirm your
   actual per-unit programme costs.
7. **Tax exemption** — confirmation of your 80G and 12A status, so the relevant
   wording and registration numbers can be added to the Donate page.
8. **Geographic footprint** — the states shown on the Our Impact map are
   indicative. Please confirm where you actually operate.
9. **Press coverage** — the Press page currently states that coverage will be
   listed as it happens. For each item we need the date, the outlet, the
   headline, a one-line summary and a link.
10. **Office hours** — the Contact page shows Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.
    Please confirm.

Items 1 through 4 and 6 through 10 can all be entered by your team directly in
the CMS. For photographs, upload them to the media library and we are happy to
place them for you if that is easier.
