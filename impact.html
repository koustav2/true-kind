Methodology

How we measure this

A number is only useful if you can see how it was built.

  1. Step one

    Tracked at intake and exit

    Every participant is recorded when they join and followed up at defined milestones afterward — not counted once at the start and quietly forgotten.

  2. Step two

    Reviewed twice a year

    The board reviews program numbers every six months, including the ones that missed target. Those get discussed first, not last.

  3. Step three

    Reported, not just tallied

    Our annual report publishes the methodology behind each figure, so a funder can see how a number was built rather than taking it on faith.
